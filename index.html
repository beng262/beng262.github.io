B-Agent: nine AI products, one platform.

B-Agent is UnifyTech's family of AI agents for businesses. Each app does one job well, they all share Microsoft sign-in, and companies switch them on per seat from one workspace. I built them end to end: the product, the AI, the data pipelines and the cloud.

Screens are from the live apps, in Turkish, the main market. People, companies and tenant figures in them are replaced with made-up ones.

How I build

AI people can trust with real work.

Demos are easy. Software a law firm or an HR team relies on every day needs a few rules that never bend.

01

The model reads. Code decides.

In Efor the AI only reads messy Excel files and sentences. Dates, hours, totals and every database write are tested code, so a number is never made up.

02

Cite it or drop it.

In Law every case number in an answer is checked against the index before you see it, and anything that can't be verified is removed.

03

Your data stays yours.

Research and Law encrypt each person's chats and files with their own key. Admins see usage counts, never the words.

04

AI proposes. People confirm.

Drafted effort rows, file edits on your own computer, follow-up actions: nothing is saved until a person clicks yes.
