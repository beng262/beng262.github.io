The problem

B-Agent grew into many separate apps, each on its own subdomain with its own sign-in. Companies needed one place to find the products, sign in once and control which colleagues use which app.

The approach

One multi-tenant Entra app across every bagent.com.tr site, plus a small licensing control plane that stores seats per company and per product. The marketing site and the signed-in workspace share their content, and product apps check the same tokens and entitlements.

Hard problems

What took real engineering.

Problem 1

One sign-in across many subdomains

Single-page apps cannot register wildcard redirect URIs, so every product origin gets the same small redirect bridge page, registered on one multi-tenant Entra app. Users are identified by tenant id plus object id in every app, and the API accepts a list of audiences, so a product with its own app registration still fits. Customers consent once, and every product validates the same issuer.

Problem 2

Two kinds of seats

Workspace seats cap how many people a company has; product seats cap how many of them can use a given product. A product with zero seats was not bought, so it cannot be assigned and never appears after sign-in, and limits are enforced both on save and at sign-in. Companies created before per-product licensing count as unlimited, so introducing it locked nobody out, and the last tenant admin can never be removed.
